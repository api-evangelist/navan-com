Business travelers can simultaneously earn rewards from three independent systems—airline loyalty programs, credit card travel multipliers, and booking platform rewards—on a single trip. Most travelers only capture one layer, leaving hundreds of dollars in annual value unclaimed. Navan Edge enables "triple-layer stacking" by offering 5% back in Amazon Gift Cards on hotel stays, stacking automatically with airline miles and credit card points.
