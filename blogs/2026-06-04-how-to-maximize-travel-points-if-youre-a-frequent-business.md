Frequent business travelers can significantly increase rewards by consolidating loyalty programs into single chains (unlocking 50-75% point bonuses), using the "triple-dip strategy" to earn from loyalty programs, credit cards, and booking platforms simultaneously, and reducing points leakage from uncredited stays. Navan Edge's Loyalty Wallet aggregates airline, hotel, and credit card rewards in one unified dashboard to help travelers track progress toward elite status tiers.
